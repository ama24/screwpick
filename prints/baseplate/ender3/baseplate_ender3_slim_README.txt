sortering-pnp baseplate for the Creality Ender 3 / Pro / V2 (slim)
================================================================

The 5 x 5 grid with a flat border out to the bed's edges (235 x 235 mm), so it can be
clamped down with binder clips like a glass plate. It is cut on cell lines into 4 pieces
that each print on this printer: lay them out as named (front-left at the bed's front-left
corner) and put at least one clip on every outer edge of every piece.

235 x 235 mm aluminium bed (or glass on it); clamp the edges with binder clips.
The grid's front-left corner lies 12.5 mm from the left edge and 12.5 mm from the front;
Resync finds it anyway. Corner notches (10 mm) clear the bed's corner screws.
Slim (2.5 mm) or standard (4.65 mm): both hold the bins the same way.
