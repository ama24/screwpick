screwpick baseplate for the Prusa i3 MK3 / MK3S (MK52 bed) (slim)
===================================================================

The 5 x 5 grid with a flat border out to the bed's edges (254 x 231 mm), so it can be
clamped down with binder clips like a glass plate. It is cut on cell lines into 4 pieces
that each print on this printer: lay them out as named (front-left at the bed's front-left
corner) and put at least one clip on every outer edge of every piece.

On a spring steel sheet (254 x 241 mm), best a spare one: stick the pieces onto it with double-sided tape, and the sheet snaps onto the magnetic bed in the same place every time (its pins locate it). Binder clips also work, but their jaws under the sheet keep it from lying flat. The back stays 10 mm short of the two alignment pins.
The grid's front-left corner lies 22 mm from the left edge and 10.5 mm from the front;
Resync finds it anyway. Corner notches (6 mm) clear the bed's corner screws.
Slim (2.5 mm) or standard (4.65 mm): both hold the bins the same way.
