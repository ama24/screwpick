    Calibrate

    The camera, the camera to the printer, and the picker tip

    Camera calibration

    Tells the program how the camera sees: the lens, and where it stands over the tray. sortering-pnp parks the bed itself; only the camera is screwrig's. Do it once, and again whenever the camera or the tray moved. The result lands in the Files tab by itself.

    1. The board:
      Print at 100 % (no scaling), glue it flat on stiff card, measure one square with a caliper.
    2. Lock the camera (empty tray, lights as when sorting): exposure, colours and focus are frozen for every photo.
    3. Lens: hold the board under the camera, in a different place and a little tilted (up to 10°) each time, until the whole picture is covered, corners too: 25 to 40 photos.
    4. Position: lay the board flat in the tray, on the TPU sheet where the parts lie, away from the corner post.

    After step 4: redo Camera to printer below (the new calibration starts its millimetres at the board).

    What the model sees

    Only the tray's inside goes to the model: the bins, their codes and the tray's walls are painted over with the floor's colour, the photo keeping its size (the parts stay as large as the model learnt them). Take a photo and look: the painted edge should sit just at the foot of the tray's walls. Margin: + keeps a strip of the wall's foot (parts lying right against it stay whole), − paints more of the floor's edge (if the wall still shows).

    Camera to printer

    1. Put at least 3 bins far apart on the bed, then Take photo.
    2. Jog the picker tip onto the centre of a marker, then Tip is here on its row.
    3. Repeat for 3 or more markers, then Fit. Errors under 0.5 mm are good.
    4. Check moves the tip 2 mm above a marker with the fitted calibration.

    Print bins

    Bins, trays, baseplates and holders, ready to print

    Bin sizes

    Each size has its own range of codes, so the camera knows a bin's size from its code, and whether the code sits on a shelf along the back or on a post in the back-right corner (codes 50–99). Use a corner tray as the plate: nothing overhangs there, so no part gets trapped. Every plate is a full build plate of one size for a 220 × 220 bed: all the same height, so one colour change at the rim (21 mm for 3u, 42 mm for 6u) makes all the codes black. The same files are in the repository under prints/.

    Baseplate

    Just the grid, for this layout's size: slim (2.5 mm) · standard (4.65 mm)

    Clamped to the whole bed (the same grid with a border out to the bed's edges, for binder clips; in pieces that fit the printer):

    Creality Ender 3 235 × 235 mm, 4 piecesslim · standard
    Prusa MK3 / MK3S steel sheet 254 × 241 mm, 4 pieces, back 10 mm free for the pinsslim · standard

    Holders for the top bar

    A screen holder (7" Waveshare HDMI LCD (H): an open frame holding it by its edge, so the Pi on the display's back sits free inside) and a camera arm (Camera Module 3, lens straight down at the bed's centre with the bed all the way out), both on the printer's 2020 top bar with M5 T-nuts. Measure your printer: reach = from the bar's front face forward to the plate's centre, bed out; bar height = from the bar's underside down to the plate (the lens goes 300 mm above the plate).

    How it works

    sortering-pnp keeps its own copy of every file it uses, so it runs on its own. Fetch each one from where it says, and drop it on its card: it is checked, kept on this Pi and used at once. A file you leave out is not missing: the card says what is done without it. The models are in the Models tab.
