    Grid

    Origin: printer X/Y of the grid's front-left corner. Rules: DIN912_M4x16 (part number), DIN912_M4 (class), DIN912 (family), wildcards like DIN912_M* or *. The most specific rule wins.

    Camera to printer

    1. Put at least 3 bins (with markers) far apart on the bed, then Take photo.
    2. Jog the picker tip until it touches the centre of one marker, then Tip is here on that marker's row.
    3. Repeat for every marker (3 or more), then Fit. Errors under 0.5 mm are good.
    4. Check moves the tip 2 mm above a marker using the fitted calibration.

    Printable files

    Everything for the current layout: screwpick_print.zip (all bins, the baseplate, paper labels, notes) · baseplate.stl · labels.svg

    Bins come as 3MF with two parts: bin (white) and marker (black). Multi-colour printer: give each its filament. One extruder: print white and add a colour change at the rim height (height units × 7 mm, 21 mm for 3u), so only the raised marker cells come out black. STL = one colour (then glue a paper label on the shelf).
