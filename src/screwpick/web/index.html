        Sorting

        Wild bins have no rules: the first part of a new kind takes a free one, and it collects that kind until it is emptied. Bins with rules come first: DIN912_M4x16, DIN912_M4, DIN912, or wildcards like DIN912_M*.

        Baseplate

        Lay the baseplate anywhere on the bed and put the bins where you like: Resync finds all of it and makes the biggest bin the plate. To choose another plate, give a bin the role work; Resync then keeps your choice. The baseplate for this size is in Print bins.

        Camera to printer

        1. Put at least 3 bins far apart on the bed, then Take photo.
        2. Jog the picker tip onto the centre of a marker, then Tip is here on its row.
        3. Repeat for 3 or more markers, then Fit. Errors under 0.5 mm are good.
        4. Check moves the tip 2 mm above a marker with the fitted calibration.

        Bin sizes

        Each size has its own range of codes, so the camera knows a bin's size from its code, and whether the code sits on a shelf along the back or on a post in the back-right corner (codes 50–99). Use a corner tray as the plate: nothing overhangs there, so no part gets trapped. Every plate is a full build plate of one size for a 220 × 220 bed: all the same height, so one colour change at the rim (21 mm for 3u, 42 mm for 6u) makes all the codes black. The same files are in the repository under prints/.

        Holders for the top bar

        A screen holder (7" Waveshare HDMI LCD (H), the Pi 4 on its back) and a camera arm (Camera Module 3, lens straight down at the bed's centre with the bed all the way out), both on the printer's 2020 top bar with M5 T-nuts. Measure your printer: reach = from the bar's front face forward to the plate's centre, bed out; bar height = from the bar's underside down to the plate (the lens goes 300 mm above the plate).
